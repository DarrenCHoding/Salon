01 The Studio

We took everything unnecessary out of the salon, and what's left is the hair.

MONO began with a simple observation: most salons are loud, whether in colour, in noise or in upsell. We built the opposite. A calm, gallery-like space on Ossington where the work is considered, the products are few and exceptional, and every appointment begins with listening.

Our stylists train in London and Tokyo methods of precision cutting, and our colour room works exclusively tone-on-tone, choosing dimension over noise. You leave with hair that grows out as beautifully as it was cut.

03 The Ritual

Every appointment,
three movements.

  1. 01

    The Consultation

    We start seated, hair dry, in front of the mirror. How you wear it, how it behaves at 7am, what you've loved and hated. Then we agree on a plan and a price before a drop of water touches your hair.

  2. 02

    The Craft

    Precision cutting on natural fall. Colour built tone-on-tone in our daylight colour room. No timers rushed, no double-booked chairs, because your appointment is yours alone.

  3. 03

    The Care

    You leave with a two-minute routine, not a bag of products, plus a complimentary fringe or neckline tidy between visits. Good hair should survive the salon door.
